Investing in Our Planet

What is GEF?

The Global Environment Facility (GEF) is the world's largest multilateral fund dedicated to protecting the environment and supporting sustainable development. Established in 1992, GEF helps countries address global environmental challenges through grants and partnerships.

What does GEF do?

🌿 Biodiversity Protection

Protecting ecosystems, wildlife and genetic diversity.

☀ Climate Action

Supporting renewable energy and climate resilience.

💧 Land & Water Management

Restoring landscapes and improving water security.

♻ Pollution Reduction

Reducing harmful chemicals and waste.

🤝 Capacity Building

Strengthening institutions and communities.
